💌 My Heart, For You

Nenu ninnu na life lo highest place lo pettukunna — the place where my parents are. ❤️

Ninnu chala sarlu teliyakundane baadha pettanu, hurt chesanu. I am genuinely sorry for every one of those moments. Once I realised my mistakes, I promised myself that I would try my level best not to repeat them again.

I will always respect your perspective, your feelings, and the way you see things — even when it hurts me sometimes. Because loving you, for me, is not about changing you. It is about understanding you, respecting you, and standing beside you.

When I pray, I include you in every prayer. Somewhere in my heart, I always pray that you should be happy, peaceful, and become the best version of yourself. And if I can be a part of that journey, I will consider myself lucky. ❤️

Nuvvu neeku nachinattu undu. Ninnu eppudu support chese vallalo nenu front lo unta.

No one can replace you.
No one can change the place you have in me. ❤️

You are my first love, and you are my last love.

I don't need someone perfect. I don't need any conditions or parameters. I just need you, till my last breath.

Nothing in this world can stop me from loving you…
not even youuu. ❤️

Nenu mee daddy antha love cheyyalekapovachu. But mee daddy taruvata, ninnu antha ga preminche people lo nenu first lo undalani korukuntunna.

And natho unnantha sepu, mee daddy love ni miss avvakunda chusukune responsibility naadhi. I swear. ❤️

If I have to wait just for us to be together in the end, then I'll patiently wait for you until my last breath.

Whatever the situation is, whatever life brings us, I will be there. I will stand by you. Not only when everything is beautiful, but especially when things are difficult.

I don't need only beautiful moments with you.

I want every moment with you — because somehow, you make even the simplest moments beautiful. ❤️

Maybe someday we will look back at all these little moments and realise that these were actually the most beautiful parts of our story.

Neepai naaku unna prema eppatiki podhu raa. ❤️

Distance may change how often we see each other. Time may change many things around us.

But you will always have a place in my heart that nobody else can take.

When you miss me, when hostel feels lonely, or when you simply need to remember how much you mean to me...

Come back to these words.

Because somewhere, someone is always praying for you, waiting for you, supporting you, and loving you.

Me. ❤️

Until my last breath.
Always yours. ❤️
